Measured by 0c on integration d8f758e6 (M landed), read only (nothing built or run), 2026-09-15.

S2, by the design (LOCK_REMOVAL_DESIGN.txt 189-203 on d6/lock-removal 0efb2732): capabilities instead of address
lookups inside the runtime, locking unchanged. "Every call site of the spine's C1, C2 and C5 takes the record its
caller already holds": run_one and the context worker use the take's record (C2); bind, map, unbind, settle and step
use the parent's child pointer (C1); R0's sweep uses R0's child list (C5). C3 (send) and C4 (the child's liveness
query) change in S4. Green: the C1/C2/C5 lookup counts at 0 and the inventories' C1/C2/C5 lines gone. The classes
and their functions are LOCK_REMOVAL_SPINE.txt 27-46 (d6/lock-removal) and b5's LOCK_REMOVAL_S2_LOOKUPS.txt
(l2src/, 88 sites in 69 functions: C1 27, C2 18, C3 9, C5 3, C4 6, Q 1, X 2, N 22), cited below as SPINE and
LOOKUPS. Neither names a public signature change: whether an entry point a test calls by address (map_child,
exec_bind, dispose_child, end_turn, run_entry_turn ...) keeps its address parameter and resolves from the caller's
record, or is retyped to take the record, is the lead's form. So "asserts" below means the gate calls, or pins the
text of, a C1/C2/C5 entry point by address, at the line named: the conversion must look at each.
