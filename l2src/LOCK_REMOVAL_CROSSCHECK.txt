--- 1. LmxMsgExec.nworkers -- disagreement

S5 (LOCK_REMOVAL_S5_LISTS.txt, field: nworkers): "DEL, M -- once each L3
  Thread is its own OS thread with no shared launch/reap bookkeeping, a
  runtime-wide worker count has nothing left to count"
F (LmxMsgExec.nworkers, c:94): "count of context workers in their loops
  (S3: stop's rounds read it) | L3T of R0 (R0's children still running,
  read from their running flags) | proposed | S4"
Which is right: F's is the later, lead-ruled reading and should stand --
  it reframes nworkers not as a maintained counter that simply disappears
  (S5's reading) but as a value R0 can always derive on demand by reading
  each child's own `running` flag, which is a stronger, more specific
  claim than S5's "nothing left to count." S5's own stage (M) is not
  wrong in spirit -- once M lands there is genuinely no shared counter --
  but F's S4 is more precise: the derivation F proposes (reading each
  child's running flag) is available as soon as S4's owner-lane-writes
  discipline is real, before M's own-OS-thread change is needed. Not
  flagged as "the lead decides" since F's own text already reads like a
  considered answer, not an open question.
