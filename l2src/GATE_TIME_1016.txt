  The 1016 s run did the same work as the 510 s and 520 s runs, and took about 2.2
  times as long in every translating and compiling gate from about 04:53 to 05:09.

  repository trees or the session temp directories, so these logs do not name it;
  they rule out a pinned-translator rebuild, a single cold compile, a wait, a second
