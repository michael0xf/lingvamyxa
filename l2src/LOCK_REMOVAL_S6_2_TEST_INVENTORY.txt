  nothing meaningful once retain/release are gone; if the scenario it

  coverage, it is the redirect-and-refuse case named below, not this one.

  refs before a copy-allocation-OOM send; asserts refs unchanged and
  inbox still 0 after the failed send. Fate: restated -- drop the refs

  liveness check (the destination is still findable/not disposed) if
  the inbox check alone does not already imply it; the property being
  tested ("a failed send does not corrupt or prematurely settle the
  destination") does not need a count.

6156-6180, "exec outbox-gone" (refs at 6157/6172): refs0 before, refs1
  after the destination stops mid-outbox-take; asserts `refs1 > refs0 +
  1` is false (a loose upper bound, not equality) plus inbox still 0.
  Fate: restated -- drop the refs bound, keep the inbox-0 check, add a
  state check (the destination reached STOPPED, or is otherwise
  observably settled) in its place; the bound was a proxy for "no
  leaked extra retain," which restates as "no leaked extra anything,"
  best expressed as the destination's own state/handoff_ready reaching
  its expected value.
