# Stage M: first read of the stepped tests (grep on d6/lock-s2-red = cf326822 + counter; not yet decided)

The rule (design 997de734): a property of the sequential mapping itself (round-robin order, the cursor, the UI take)
is deleted; a property of the child (its close, its settle, its mail, its collection) moves onto the child's own
thread through map_child.
The rule for every conversion (the coordinator, 2026-09-15, recorded in the plan): a converted test states its
property through Messages, turns and flag reads, never through a wait. No cell a turn blocks on, no holding a turn
inside its body until the test releases it: that is a semaphore-shaped handoff, outside the Message frame for a core
test. An ordering claim between two lanes is not made; what one lane has done is observed by reading the other
Message's flags (running, success, handoff_safe) at the moment the acting call returns, and a later effect is read on
later rounds.
