LOCK_REMOVAL_A_ALLOCS: every allocation and free in l2src outside the Message's
own arena (lmx_msg_turn_*, the blocks/ranges growth mechanism), at integration
c063fd00, against the A-stage design (LOCK_REMOVAL_DESIGN.txt A1-A3, line 862
onward) -- one arena per Message, arenas attached at consumption (sonnet/a-allocs,
2026-09-15, doc-only, no machine).

Design, quoted (LOCK_REMOVAL_DESIGN.txt:862-876). Mikhail on 5.4, verbatim:
"какая копия? У нас нет никаких копий message... Есть только он сам и у него
своя арена. Пускай это будет один alloc но это его собственный. При получении
Message который не L3 Thread присоединяется к арене получателя." (no copies of
a Message; it has only itself and its own arena; one alloc, but its own; a
received Message that is not an L3 Thread joins the recipient's arena.)
  A1. The Message's control record (LmxMsg, today calloc in slot_new) becomes a
      Structure in its own arena, created on the launching parent's lane.
  A2. The arena's bookkeeping (LmxOwnedRange and LmxMsgBlock records) and the
      Message's own arrays (path, init, done_*, roots) are allocated from that
      arena.
  A3. An admitted copy's arena (5.4). Mailbox entries (LmxMsgCopy) follow his
      answer: the envelope copy made at admission is today's artefact and goes.
