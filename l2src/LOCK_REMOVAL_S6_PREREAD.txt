--- Note on lmx_msg_pump (the one row this document does not count either way)

lmx_msg_pump's own exec_lock, around rt->transport's pop, is deliberately
NOT resolved to DELETE or CONTRADICTION here. transport/transport_tail's
fate is S5/Y3 (LOCK_REMOVAL_S5_LISTS.txt), not S6: host_post admits into
this queue from potentially many concurrent external callers (unlike
every DELETE row above, whose writer is a single already-established
lane), and pump drains it. Per Mikhail's own standing rule (no locks
except the mailbox collection), this lock's own future is to become part
of -- or be subsumed by -- the mailbox's own synchronized structure once
Y's own design lands ("admissions become direct admissions into R0's own
mailbox," per LOCK_REMOVAL_S5_LISTS.txt's own words), not to be deleted
outright at S6 alongside the executor lock's other sites. Counting it
under DELETE would misstate what happens to it; counting it as a
CONTRADICTION would wrongly imply a gap in today's ownership work, when
the real situation is that it is out of S6's own scope by design. Left
named, not counted, so the two counts above stay accurate for what they
actually claim.
