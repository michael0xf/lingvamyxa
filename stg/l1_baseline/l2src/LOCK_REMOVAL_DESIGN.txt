WHAT THE LOCK DOES TODAY (from the inventories, not from a rule)
L1. Address resolution. A walk from rt->root through every child list turns an LmxMsgAddr into a record
    (msg_find_any_locked, lmx_msg_find, self_or_find). The lock keeps the tree still under the walk. This is 35 of
    exec.c's 41 silent 5.1 lines.
L2. The family tree's own writes: child_link, child_chain_remove, the orphan re-rooting, the root-list unlink in
    endp_try_retire and release_slot.
L3. The execution mapping's cells: exec_bind record fields, mapped, launching, the bind wait's generation.
L4. Control flags read and written across lanes: ready, closing, running, handoff_ready, native_users.
L5. Runtime-level lists: rt->slots/n, e->retire_head, e->reap_head, rt->transport, e->ui_lane.
L6. Wakes: exec_wake_locked signals every worker after any turn; exec_ready routes one wake.
L7. Storage moves at settle: adopt_mark, dispose_mark, transfer_adopted, transfer_graph.
L8. Test setters and counters.
