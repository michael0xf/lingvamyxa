5. Open, deliberately - do NOT close these in code

    POINTER CONTRAST. A filled white arrow over a light panel is invisible and
      these glyphs have no outline. Build it, look at it, then ask. UI_MODEL 6.3.
    MISSING GLYPH, and U+2B95 specifically - the rightwards arrow was added to
      Unicode later than its three siblings and is the likeliest of the eight to
      be absent. Report per platform before designing a fallback. UI_MODEL 6.3.
    TWO VIEWPORTS AT ONCE. Moving the highlight into a list that is off to the
      side must scroll the ROW horizontally and the LIST vertically. Both follow
      from one rule but they are two scrolls and the order is visible to the
      user. UI_MODEL 5.7.2.
    WHICH CELLS a highlight frame costs on a SQUARE button. Answered in
      principle - it overlays, nothing is displaced - but it wants looking at.
      DRAWING 5.4.2.


6. Found today and not yet reported upstream

L1 cannot spell `const T *const *` as a return type: nested const in the paren
form yields `const const T **`. grok_bot worked around it with a 34-line
hand-written C file, mixa_backend_tables.c, holding the registry array, and
documented the reason at the top of it.

Two things follow and neither is done:
    the workaround is hand-written C inside mixa_manager, where until now only
      HEADERS were hand-written. That is a real exception to the hermeticity the
      distribution model asks for and it should be either accepted in writing or
      removed once L1 can express the type.
    it may be an L1 defect worth reporting - but verify against l1src before
      reporting anything, because a "defect" the self-hosted translator relies on
      is load-bearing.


7. Standing constraints, unchanged

    the specification is mixa_manager/. Everything discussed goes into it,
      immediately, and is committed before it is ticketed. README.txt has the
      file list and the standing rule.
    do not rebuild or replace gen2, do not run stg/l1_baseline/gate.ps1 for
      app-only work, do not touch l1src, tests/l1, L1_spec, stg/l1_baseline
      sources, or another agent's working files.
    never edit mixa_manager/clearshell_reference/ - it is a snapshot of
      Mikhail's other project and the source of truth for the port.
    binaries are not committed. A runnable artefact is COPIED to
      mixa_manager/bin, whose contents are untracked.
    the stable translator is stg/l1_baseline/build/l1trans/gen2/l1trans.exe,
      SHA256 65D5A5ED as of 01:37 today. It is NOT the same binary as
      build/l1trans/gen2 in the root tree - probing the wrong one cost a set of
      wrong attributions earlier today.
