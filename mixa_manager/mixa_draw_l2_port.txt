Two real bugs found and fixed in the harness itself (not in mixa_draw)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both caught by simply running the harness once against the real oracle
before ever attempting the L2 side or committing -- neither is a defect
in mixa_draw.lm1/.lm2 itself:
  1. The first draft's own draw_fill call passed (fg=1, bg=2, alpha=200)
     but the check asserted bg==200 -- a plain parameter-order mistake
     in the test, not the module. Fixed by reordering the call to
     (fg=1, bg=200, alpha=255) so the >=128 sign-extension coverage
     (already established for tiles/text_rect) is preserved correctly
     against the field it actually exercises.
  2. The first draft's own SCN 6 drew a horizontal line, then a crossing
     vertical line, and expected a plain T-junction where they merely
     crossed -- but had not correctly traced through mixa_draw_merge_
     cell's own TWO DIFFERENT branches: a stub landing on an EMPTY cell
     (old_mask=0) takes the "expand a lone stub to a full axis segment"
     path (mixa_draw_expand_singles), while a stub landing on an
     EXISTING box character takes the "union the two stub sets" path.
     The vline's own first cell was empty when drawn, so its lone DOWN
     stub silently expanded to a full vertical bar (U+D) before the two
     lines' own intended crossing point was even reached, making the
     actual, correct result a full 4-way cross (0x253C), not a T-shape.
     Rebuilt as an unambiguous scenario instead: draw the frame FIRST
     (so its edge cells already hold real box content, mask 12) then
     cross it with a line -- this reliably lands in the "union" branch
     and produces genuine, correctly-predicted T-junctions, which is
     what actually got committed.
